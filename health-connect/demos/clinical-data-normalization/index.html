Normalize clinical data without guessing.

See Health-Connect test facility-scoped rules before publication, measure PIQI lift field by field, quarantine uncertain records for expert review, and turn approved local-code decisions into reusable FHIR ConceptMaps.

Demo environment This recording uses synthetic fixture data and demonstrates current product behavior; it does not represent a live customer deployment.

A governed path from imperfect feeds to reusable knowledge.

Clinical data normalization becomes risky when a system silently invents meaning. Health-Connect separates predictable, testable repairs from decisions that require clinical or terminology expertise.

Integration engineers can dry-run a ruleset and inspect every before-and-after field. Records that cross the PIQI gate move to a review queue instead of disappearing. When a subject-matter expert approves a local-code mapping, that decision becomes a reusable FHIR ConceptMap for future messages.

Transcript

0:02Every EMR sends flawed data—missing genders, local codes, and OID identifiers. Health-Connect repairs these predictable defects right after parsing, with facility-scoped normalization rulesets.

0:19In the workbench, the integration engineer tests a ruleset against a real resource before publishing anything. Nothing is written during a dry run.

0:30Each rule hit shows the field before and after, and the PIQI quality score moves—so the quality lift is measured, not assumed.

0:43Records the quality gate holds land in a clinical review queue. Nothing is silently dropped. A subject-matter expert releases what is safe and discards what is not, and every decision is audited.

0:58When a mapping rule meets a local code with no ConceptMap entry, it flags it instead of guessing. The expert authors the mapping once, and every future message translates automatically.

1:12The mapping is written to the facility ConceptMap in the FHIR server and the flag resolves—local lab code to LOINC, permanently.

1:25Workbench-authored rules plus expert-curated mappings mean each facility's feed gets cleaner over time—and every downstream measure, agent, and dashboard benefits.
