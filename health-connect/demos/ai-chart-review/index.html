Clinical workflow demo

AI chart review from connected FHIR data.

See Health-Connect generate audience-specific clinical summaries from the same patient timeline—giving nursing, hospitalists, surgery, emergency care, and rehab teams the format they need without rebuilding the record.

1 minute 58 seconds Captions included Synthetic demo data
Demo environment This recording uses synthetic fixture data and demonstrates current product behavior; it does not represent a live customer deployment.

One record, five clinical viewpoints.

Clinical handoffs are not interchangeable. An SBAR nursing handoff needs different emphasis than a hospitalist sign-out or surgical brief. Health-Connect uses the connected FHIR timeline to assemble the relevant context, then applies the selected practice-area format.

The resulting snapshot keeps the input window, output format, and run usage visible so teams can review both the clinical content and the operational cost of generation.
