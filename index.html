Healthcare workflow intelligence

Turn connected clinical data into operational action.

Health-Connect is an intelligence layer for quality programs, compliance readiness, documentation-sensitive workflows, and provider-facing decision support. It helps teams move from normalized data to prioritized action.

Built for providers, health systems, and interoperability partners that need actionable outputs inside existing environments. evrConnect extends targeted mobile assessment workflows as the companion iOS app.

What it delivers

Operational intelligence teams can use without adopting a brand-new workflow layer.

  • Gap identification, prioritization, and measure tracking across major quality and reporting workflows.
  • Documentation, revenue integrity, and compliance-ready review support for specialized operational teams.
  • Transportable, standards-based outputs that can fit into existing portals, dashboards, and connected systems.
Best fit Hospitals, health systems, ambulatory and care management teams, plus providers using athenahealth through a provider-authorized SMART on FHIR connection. Health-Connect also supports AI chat-based integration onboarding for provider teams connecting clinical workflows into existing environments.
